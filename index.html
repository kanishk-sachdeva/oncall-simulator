How it works

  1. Three or four options per decision. One is the right move. One is what you would do at 03:00 with no sleep.
  2. Error budget drains on bad calls. Blast radius tracks the biggest group of customers a single choice touched.
  3. The runbook is available, once per incident, for −2 points and 3 minutes. Real on-call is a trade too.
  4. End a run and you get a postmortem table, a grade, and a shareable shift number.
